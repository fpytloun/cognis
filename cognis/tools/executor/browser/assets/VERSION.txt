cognis-autoconsent is a curated, self-contained CMP-killer maintained
in-tree (cognis/tools/executor/browser/assets/autoconsent.bundle.js). It
covers the major commercial CMPs (OneTrust, Cookiebot, Quantcast,
Sourcepoint, Didomi, TrustArc, Iubenda, Usercentrics, CookieYes, Borlabs,
Osano, Klaro, Termly, Moove GDPR, Complianz, CookieLawInfo) plus a
heuristic fallback for unknown banners.

Updates to selectors should be made by hand here; bump the version above
and add a CHANGELOG entry. Refresh procedure documented in
docs/guide/executors.md.
